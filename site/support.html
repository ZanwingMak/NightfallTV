How do I install it?

Join the public beta using the link below. Install TestFlight on your iPhone or iPad and Apple TV, using the same App Store account. Accept the invitation on your iPhone or iPad, then install Nightfall in TestFlight on Apple TV. TestFlight requires tvOS 18 or later. Developers can also build from GitHub.

How do I listen without a picture?

Start a video or soundscape, then choose Picture off in the player. Audio continues with a black picture. Select shows controls; Back returns to the app. Nightfall cannot turn off your TV backlight.

Can I listen without a picture?

Yes. Select a sound, then listen in absolute black or with a scene. The mixer combines up to three layers with individual levels. A sleep timer fades Nightfall’s sound; the separate picture-off timer turns the image black while the sound continues.

Where are larger downloads?

Open Settings → Download more 4K films and field recordings, or the download button in Soundscapes. Every item shows its format, size, creator and license. Downloading is optional and free. You can cancel or delete individual files. tvOS may clear caches, so check downloaded status before going offline.

What does a tip unlock?

Nothing is locked. Tips support independent development, are one-time consumable purchases and can be repeated voluntarily. Prices are provided by Apple. There is no subscription, account, restore entitlement or premium tier. If the store is unavailable, just enjoy all the free features.

Which languages are supported?

The app and this website support Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Arabic and Hindi. In the app, choose Automatic or a language in Settings. On this website, use the language menu; your choice stays in this browser.
