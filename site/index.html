BLACK SCREEN LISTENING

The best screen can be no screen.

Choose a sound first. Picture on shows a small preview with playback controls. Picture off keeps the audio playing. Stopping sound ends the session. There is no standalone video, photo gallery or clock mode.

Nightfall changes the picture, not the television’s backlight or another app’s playback.
