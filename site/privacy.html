NIGHTFALL TV / PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Effective October 12, 2026 · Developer: ZanwingMak

A quiet app, with no account

Nightfall TV is an independent, open-source Apple TV app developed by ZanwingMak. We operate no account service or developer analytics server. The app and this website contain no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not sell personal information.

What stays on your device

Language, favorites, settings, bedtime routines and photo URLs you add are stored on your Apple TV. Downloaded media are stored in the tvOS cache. This website stores only your language choice in local storage. These preferences are not sent to us. Apple may independently back up device data according to your system settings.

Downloads and photographs

Optional media are downloaded only when you choose Download. They are hosted in the public NightfallTV GitHub Releases repository. GitHub and its delivery providers receive ordinary connection information, such as IP address, time and user agent. Photo requests go directly to the HTTPS hosts you specify; those hosts may receive the same connection information and any parameters in your chosen URL. We do not proxy or upload your photos.

Payments are handled by Apple

Voluntary tips use Apple StoreKit. Apple processes payment, purchase authentication and refunds under its own privacy policies. The app verifies the transaction and completes it; we do not receive payment card details or store a purchase history on a developer server. Tips are one-time consumable purchases and do not unlock functionality.

This website and external services

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages over HTTPS, uses local fonts and assets, and sets no analytics cookies. GitHub may retain standard access logs; see docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. Apple’s policy is available at apple.com/legal/privacy. Following an external link subjects that visit to its provider’s policy.

Your choices and removal

You can delete downloads, remove photo URLs, or reset preferences, routines and photos inside Settings. Removing the app deletes its local app data, subject to Apple backup behavior. tvOS may clear cached media automatically. You can clear this website’s language choice in your browser’s site-data settings. We cannot delete records held independently by Apple, GitHub or photo hosts; contact those providers directly.

Support and changes

If you open a GitHub issue, its contents are public and processed by GitHub. Do not include passwords, payment details or private photo URLs. No personal information is required to use Nightfall. We do not knowingly collect information from children. Material policy changes will update the effective date here. Privacy questions can be raised through the project’s GitHub issues.
