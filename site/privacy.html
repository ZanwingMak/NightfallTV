NIGHTFALL SLEEP / PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Effective October 17, 2026 · Developer: ZanwingMak

A quiet app, with no account

Nightfall Sleep is an independent, open-source Apple TV app developed by ZanwingMak. We operate no account service or developer analytics server. The app and this website contain no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not sell personal information.

What stays on your device

Language, audio preferences, saved routines and downloads stay on your Apple TV. This version does not load personal photos. Reset also clears photo URLs retained from earlier versions.

Downloads

Optional downloads come directly from public GitHub Releases. GitHub and its delivery providers receive ordinary connection information such as IP address, time and user agent. tvOS may clear cached media; downloading again is free.

Payments are handled by Apple

Voluntary tips use Apple StoreKit. Apple processes payment, purchase authentication and refunds under its own privacy policies. The app verifies the transaction and completes it; we do not receive payment card details or store a purchase history on a developer server. Tips are one-time consumable purchases and do not unlock functionality.

This website and external services

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages over HTTPS, uses local fonts and assets, and sets no analytics cookies. GitHub may retain standard access logs; see docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. Apple’s policy is available at apple.com/legal/privacy. Following an external link subjects that visit to its provider’s policy.

Your choices and removal

Remove downloads or reset local data in Settings. Uninstalling removes local app data, subject to Apple backups. Clear this website’s language choice in your browser’s site-data settings. Apple and GitHub manage their own records.

Support and changes

If you open a GitHub issue, its contents are public and processed by GitHub. Do not include passwords, payment details or private photo URLs. No personal information is required to use Nightfall. We do not knowingly collect information from children. Material policy changes will update the effective date here. Privacy questions can be raised through the project’s GitHub issues.
