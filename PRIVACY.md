# 隐私说明 / Privacy

Nightfall TV 不含广告、分析、跟踪或第三方 SDK，不要求账号，不向开发者服务器发送偏好、照片或使用记录。

收藏、设置、命名睡前方案和个人照片地址保存在 Apple TV 的本地应用存储。可以在设置中重置这些数据。照片缓存只在内存中保留，不作为长期照片备份。

只有播放用户主动添加的 HTTPS 照片时才访问该照片网站。网站可能按其自己的政策记录 IP 地址和正常的访问请求。请使用你有权展示的图片，不在地址中输入私人凭据。内置画面和声景全部离线。

TestFlight 诊断与反馈由 Apple 根据其隐私政策处理，独立于本应用的功能数据。

Nightfall TV has no advertising, analytics, tracking, accounts, or third-party SDKs. Preferences, favorites and photo URLs stay in the app's local storage on your Apple TV. Reset them in Settings.

Only photo hosts explicitly chosen by you are contacted when displaying your HTTPS photographs. These hosts may log your IP address and normal requests under their own policies. Photos are cached in memory, not as permanent backups. All built-in scenes and sounds work offline. Apple's TestFlight diagnostics and feedback are handled by Apple separately.
